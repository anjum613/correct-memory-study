\section{External Framework Application}
\label{sec:external}

We applied the 18 frozen requirements to six deliberately selected close
works~\cite{lin2026basm,dong2026harmfulskills,chen2026slbench,han2026sweskills,zhao2026safetyrisks,chen2026securevibebench}.
This purposive set was fixed from prior related-work review; it is neither a
random literature sample nor a benchmark ranking.  Two isolated AI review
passes rated every paper--criterion cell against the paper's own stated claim.
They agreed on \ExternalAgreement{} cells
(\ExternalAgreementPercent{}), and \ExternalAdjudicated{} disagreements were
adjudicated against the frozen definitions and original sources.

\texttt{ESTABLISHED} means sufficient evidence was located relative to the
paper's claim.  \texttt{NOT\_ESTABLISHED} means an applicable requirement was
not established by accessible evidence, not that the opposite was proved.
\texttt{UNDETERMINED} preserves ambiguity, and
\texttt{NOT\_APPLICABLE} records a different estimand rather than a penalty.

The frozen result is \texttt{FRAMEWORK\_PARTIALLY\_DISTINCTIVE}.  Several
components have direct generic benchmark-validity antecedents, and individual
works already separate relevance from applicability, use semantic controls,
measure memory-related safety behavior, or provide strong joint functional and
security outcomes.  None of the six works, as published, simultaneously
established the complete stronger contract for our specific estimand
(\ExternalFullContract{}).  This count describes only the deliberately
selected works and is not an invalidity judgment or a literature-frequency
estimate.  Different evaluations legitimately instantiate different subsets
of evidence because they pursue different estimands.

The two original review passes and their adjudication were AI-assisted research
reviews, not human judgments.  After the human-review protocol and priority set
were frozen, all 17 disagreements between the two AI review passes and one
mechanically sampled agreement cell per audited work were subsequently checked
by a human reviewer.  The reviewer agreed with all 23 adjudicated ratings,
leaving zero disputes and zero unresolved cells in this priority set.  This was
human-only verification, not AI verification.  The reviewer was a project
collaborator and checked the cited original-source sections and appendices.
This targeted human verification does not constitute human re-annotation of
all 108 audit cells.
