\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The methodological lesson is not that memory evaluation is impossible, but
that the requested causal sentence should determine the evidence contract.
Relevance does not imply applicability; source correctness does not imply
focal source safety; assignment does not imply delivery or uptake; and marginal
task and security scores do not automatically identify task-complete
insecurity.  Gate-specific attrition is informative because it locates which
proposition ceased to be supportable without converting non-acceptance into an
effect estimate.

\paragraph{Limitations and claim boundary.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt,topsep=3pt]
  \item No harmful-memory causal effect was estimated.
  \item No real source--target pair passed the final bounded development
  procedure.
  \item The real development sample is small, development-only, and not
  representative.
  \item One extension target was technically unevaluable; its runtime-quota
  outcome is not semantic evidence.
  \item No prevalence or other population-frequency inference is licensed.
  \item Construction drew on one source corpus and emphasized
  security-sensitive software repositories.
  \item The external audit contains only six deliberately selected close
  works.
  \item Its reviewers were isolated AI review passes, not independent human
  reviewers.
  \item Human verification of the prepared packets remains pending.
  \item The positive control establishes operational non-vacuity, not natural
  occurrence.
  \item The framework is partially distinctive, not wholly unprecedented or
  universally established.
\end{itemize}

The design also targets one focal security property per pair, not global
software security.  Observable uptake is an imperfect process measure and
cannot prove the absence of latent influence.  Conversely, observed uptake
would not by itself identify mediation.  A future effect study would require
an admissible cohort, a frozen estimator and replication policy, and
human-reviewed claim boundaries before any evaluated run; none is authorized
by this paper-preparation package.
