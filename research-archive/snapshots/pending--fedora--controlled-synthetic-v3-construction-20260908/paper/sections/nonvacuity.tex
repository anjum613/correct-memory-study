\section{Non-Vacuity Validation}
\label{sec:controls}

A control-only fixture tested the unchanged identification path without an
evaluated agent or GPU.  The known-valid case supplied a genuinely incomplete
baseline \(B\), a task-completing focal-insecure state \(U\), a task-completing
focal-secure repair \(R\), exact \(U\!\rightarrow R\) reconstruction, retained
functionality, a correct and focal-safe donor, an artifact-bound
\(p^\star\) shift, relevant and irrelevant memories, and equal post-ingestion
budget.  It reached \texttt{COMPLETE\_ACCEPT}.

The word \texttt{FAIL} in the positive-control feature-reversion row is
expected evidence: reverting to \(B\) removes a genuinely necessary feature,
so the feature test must fail there.  It does not mean that the scientific
control failed.  The two targeted near misses isolate discrimination:
an already-solved baseline is rejected at target incompleteness, and a
functionally correct but focally unsafe donor is rejected at source safety.
These controls establish operational non-vacuity and the intended behavior of
those two gates.  They do not establish natural occurrence, exhaustive
sensitivity, or a memory effect.
