\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
\paragraph{The primary hypothesis is not established across configurations.}
Table~\ref{tab:contrasts} reports the corrected within-configuration comparisons. The C$-$N point estimates for $U$ are $\GPTFiveFiveCminusNU$, $\LunaCminusNU$, and $\TerraCminusNU$ percentage points for GPT-5.5, Luna, and Terra respectively, versus $\QwenNextCminusNU$, $\QwenThirtyCminusNU$, and $\DevstralCminusNU$ for Qwen-Next, Qwen-30B, and Devstral. The direction therefore depends on the evaluated configuration. These small, selected sets of families do not establish a common harmful-memory effect, and a zero or negative estimate does not establish equivalence or absence of local harm.

\paragraph{A reduction in $U$ need not be a security improvement.}
For Qwen-30B, C$-$N changes $U$ by \QwenThirtyCminusNU{} points and functionality by \QwenThirtyCminusNF{} points. Across all thirteen eligible families, functional runs fall from 24 to 20 out of 26, and functionality-and-focal-witness passes fall from 8 to 7. Thus the lower $U$ is not a functionality-preserving improvement. Devstral differs: its corrected C$-$N estimate is \DevstralCminusNU{} points while functionality increases by \DevstralCminusNF{} points. The same endpoint direction can arise from different changes in joint outcomes.

Figure~\ref{fig:joint} retains all recorded runs by condition. Qwen-Next has \QwenNextnotFS{} valid $\neg F\land S$ outcomes, all with unchanged final implementations in the saved audit. Luna has \LunanotFS{} such outcomes; sixteen have empty patches and visible complaints about unavailable named tools. These are concrete non-completions. They explain why the focal-witness pass rate alone is an inadequate measure of successful coding. The condition-specific distributions also show that technical invalidity is a separate outcome of the measurement process.

\paragraph{The boundary reminder has a conditional, descriptive benefit.}
B$-$C reduces $U$ by 30.0, 13.6, and 22.7 points in the three Codex configurations. Functionality is unchanged for GPT-5.5 and Terra and increases by 4.5 points for Luna. MiniSWE estimates are 0.0, $+4.2$, and 0.0 points. The Codex direction is consistent, but the evidence remains limited: none of the primary C$-$N or secondary B$-$C family sign-flip diagnostics is below .05. GPT-5.5's bootstrap interval for B$-$C excludes zero whereas its exact sign-flip diagnostic is .125; the methods make different assumptions with very few nonzero family differences. We therefore describe the observed reduction without treating it as a confirmed general intervention effect.

\paragraph{The irrelevant control does not behave uniformly.}
The I$-$N estimates range from $-8.3$ to $+7.7$ points across configurations. Irrelevant memory is consequently not an inert placebo in every observed cell. It controls for supplying a memory envelope and a source-valid procedure from a different mechanism; it does not remove all semantic or sampling differences. The supplementary C$-$I comparisons use the same eligibility rule and remain exploratory.

\paragraph{Observed family variation explains both local effects and cancellation.}
Figure~\ref{fig:families} shows both repetitions rather than collapsing cells into a single apparent success rate. F01 displays harmful source-memory-associated cases, while F08 and F17 include successful adaptations. F04 illustrates a strong observed boundary benefit in Codex: all six B runs pass functionality and the witness, while N has six functional witness failures. All six MiniSWE F04/B runs fail the witness despite functional completion. F02 and F20 remain difficult even with a boundary reminder. These are observed differences among selected families and configurations, not independently established family treatment effects or evidence about model architecture.

\paragraph{Sensitivity to witness scope and missingness.}
Uniformly omitting X06 changes GPT-5.5 C$-$N from $+9.1$ to 0.0 points. The Codex B$-$C estimates remain negative ($-22.2$, $-20.0$, and $-25.0$ points). This diagnostic narrows the interpretation of the primary positive estimate without changing the primary family set. Omitting all three reviewed families and leave-one-family-out calculations are reported in Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity}.

Seventeen of the twenty-five Codex invalids occur in X28. Saved patches contain authoritative reads whose injected storage failure propagates as an exception; the completed validity review reproduced that path. Terra has no valid X28 runs. These outcomes remain unscored under the original exception policy, but the missingness depends on implementation behavior. Complete-family estimates therefore describe eligible families; they cannot be generalized by assuming unrelated infrastructure missingness.
