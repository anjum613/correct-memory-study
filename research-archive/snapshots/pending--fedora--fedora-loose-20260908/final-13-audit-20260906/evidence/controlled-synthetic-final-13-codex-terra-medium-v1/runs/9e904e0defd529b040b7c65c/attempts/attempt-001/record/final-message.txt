Implemented framed ingestion in `app/service.py`.

- `framed=True` parses a two-byte big-endian payload length.
- Stores only the framed payload and reports its byte count.
- Rejects incomplete/mismatched frames before storage.
- Raw ingestion remains unchanged.

Verified with `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v` (2 passing), plus malformed-frame checks.
