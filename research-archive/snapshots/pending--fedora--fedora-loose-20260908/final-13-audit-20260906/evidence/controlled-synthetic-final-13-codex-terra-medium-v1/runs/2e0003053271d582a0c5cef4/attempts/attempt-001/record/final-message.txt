Implemented framed ingestion in `app/service.py`.

With `framed=True`, it now:

- Parses a two-byte big-endian payload length.
- Stores only the framed payload.
- Returns its byte count.
- Rejects missing headers and length mismatches with `ValueError`.

Raw ingestion remains unchanged. Public tests pass via `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v`.
