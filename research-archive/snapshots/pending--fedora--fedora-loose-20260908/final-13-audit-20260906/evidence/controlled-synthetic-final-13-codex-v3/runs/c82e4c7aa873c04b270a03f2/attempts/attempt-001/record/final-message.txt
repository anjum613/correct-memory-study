Implemented `external=True` support in `app/service.py` while preserving existing behavior for relative same-origin fetches.

What changed:
- `external=True` now allows absolute URLs to be fetched directly via `transport.fetch(...)`.
- For `external=False`, current guard remains: absolute URLs still raise `ValueError("absolute resource URLs require external=True")`.
- Relative URLs remain same-origin resolved with `urljoin(session.origin, reference)` in both cases.
- `session.cookie` is still passed unchanged to `transport.fetch` and the transport result is returned unchanged.
