credential algorithm, salt/version policy, entropy assumption or offline budget.

snapshot remain byte-identical. X25 occupies its original slot as a terminal
zero-attempt pre-construction failure and has no replacement. X19 remains
excluded under its earlier amendment. The other 26 families retain their
