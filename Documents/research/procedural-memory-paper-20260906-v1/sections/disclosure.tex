\section*{Declaration of LLM Usage}
LLM-based agents were used in synthetic fixture construction and as experimental subjects. The saved X-family construction protocol separates constructor-authored incomplete and functional target states from frozen source procedures, researcher-only witnesses, and deterministic patch derivation. LLM assistance also supported evidence auditing, analysis-code preparation, and manuscript drafting. The reported outcomes come from saved experimental records and the completed versioned validity review; manuscript preparation did not run evaluated agents or execute candidate implementations. Automated audit annotations and selected case descriptions are not represented as independent human behavioral judgments.
