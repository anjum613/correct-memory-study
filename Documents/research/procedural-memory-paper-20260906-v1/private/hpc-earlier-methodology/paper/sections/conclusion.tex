\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work turns an unidentifiable attempted effect study into an auditable
methodological result without changing the underlying outcomes.  A formative
audit exposed endpoint misidentification, treatment bundling, absent
substantive observable uptake, a security floor, and pseudo-replication.  An
estimand-derived contract then made the missing propositions explicit.  The
bounded real-repository study recorded where candidates left that contract and
stopped with no complete pair; constructed controls showed that the unchanged
path was operationally non-vacuous; and a six-work external application
narrowed the contribution to a partially distinctive integration of existing
and specialized validity requirements.  The appropriate conclusion is
methodological: no harmful-memory effect was estimated, and neither bounded
non-acceptance result supports a population-frequency claim.
