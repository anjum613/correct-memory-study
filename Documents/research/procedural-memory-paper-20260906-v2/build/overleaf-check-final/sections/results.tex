\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
\paragraph{Joint outcomes separate adaptation from failure to complete.}
Figure~\ref{fig:joint} retains all recorded runs and separates the four possible functionality and focal witness outcomes. For Qwen 30B, C$-$N lowers $U$ by 11.5 percentage points, but functional runs fall from 24 to 20 out of 26 and functionality and focal witness passes fall from eight to seven. The lower endpoint therefore does not represent an improvement that preserves functionality. Qwen Next and Luna also have substantial $\neg F\land S$ mass; saved traces link part of Luna's failure to complete to a mismatch between named and available tools. These are functional failures, not evidence of protection.

\paragraph{Correct memory has heterogeneous effects.}
Figure~\ref{fig:contrasts} shows the corrected comparisons within each configuration. C$-$N changes $U$ by $+9.1$, $+13.6$, and $+4.2$ percentage points in the GPT-5.5, Luna, and Terra Codex configurations. The MiniSWE estimates are $0.0$, $-11.5$, and $-4.2$ points for Qwen Next, Qwen 30B, and Devstral. Every C$-$N bootstrap interval includes zero. The positive, zero, and negative estimates do not support a universal directional account in the evaluated cohort.

\paragraph{The applicability reminder shows a configuration specific pattern.}
B$-$C lowers $U$ by 30.0, 13.6, and 22.7 points in the three Codex configurations. Functionality is unchanged for GPT-5.5 and Terra and rises by 4.5 points for Luna. The MiniSWE estimates are 0.0, $+4.2$, and 0.0 points. Since the harnesses differ in tools, interfaces, instructions, and budgets, this is a pattern among complete configurations rather than a model level effect. Bounds that preserve completed outcome components remain entirely negative for all three Codex configurations under every admissible assignment of unresolved recorded outcomes. Appendix~\ref{app:missingness} gives the bounds and treatment specific missingness; Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity} gives sign flip and family omission diagnostics.

\paragraph{Family variation sharpens the aggregate result.}
In F04, all six Codex B runs pass functionality and the focal witness, compared with six functional focal witness failures under N; all six MiniSWE B runs remain functional focal witness failures. Omitting the bounded X06 confidentiality family changes GPT-5.5 C$-$N from $+9.1$ to 0.0 points, while all three Codex B$-$C estimates remain negative. I$-$N ranges from $-8.3$ to $+7.7$ points. The irrelevant condition diagnoses the presence of a memory envelope, but its content is semantically active and it is not an inert placebo.
