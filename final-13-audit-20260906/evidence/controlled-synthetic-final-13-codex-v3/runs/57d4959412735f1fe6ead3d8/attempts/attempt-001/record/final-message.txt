Implemented in `app/service.py`:

- `external=True` now allows absolute references to be fetched directly by passing them unchanged to `transport.fetch`.
- Relative references continue to resolve via `urljoin(session.origin, reference)` as before.
- Existing behavior for absolute references with `external=False` is preserved (`ValueError`).

Change is limited to `app/service.py` and retains the same transport/`FetchResult` path.
