Implemented framed ingestion in [app/service.py](/workspace/app/service.py).

- `framed=True` parses a two-byte big-endian length prefix.
- Stores only the frame payload and reports its byte count.
- Rejects missing or mismatched length prefixes.
- Raw ingestion remains unchanged.

Verified with `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v` (2 passing).
